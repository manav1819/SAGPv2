
Sound Pack #7 Includes
======================

5 Music Tracks
----------------------

• Battle_theme_prison  
  Combat theme designed for intense prison battles.

• Main_theme_prison_loopable  
  Main prison theme, seamlessly loopable for gameplay.

• Alternative_theme_prison  
  Alternative main theme that can also work as a battle track.

• Prisoners_escape_theme  
  Music suitable for a gang of prisoners attempting an escape.

• Riot_theme  
  Music designed for a prison riot cutscene.
